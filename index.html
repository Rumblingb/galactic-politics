How to Play

Three swipes. One goal: build the world's most strategically dominant cabinet.

→

Swipe Right — Draft

Add the politician to your cabinet. Their promise score, market odds, and momentum all count toward your total points.

←

Swipe Left — Pass

Skip this politician. They hit the transfer market — someone else picks up the volatility bag.

↑

Swipe Up — Captain

One captain per cabinet. They get a 1.8× point multiplier. Use it on your highest market-heat pick.

📋

Truth Tax

Every broken promise reduces your integrity score. High truth tax tanks your league ranking — pick carefully.

🏆

World League

Your 5-card cabinet competes globally. Score = (promise × odds × momentum) − truth tax.

📰

Live Receipts

Every politician has a promise receipt log. Wild card events can spike or crash your cabinet score mid-season.
